About us

A legacy built on trust and technology

Shortt Legacy Group OPC is an IT consulting and technology solutions company based in Arakkonam, Tamil Nadu. Led by Savia Bernadeth Shortt (Middleton), we help businesses choose the right technology, build what’s missing and keep everything running smoothly.

The “Legacy” in our name is a promise: to build solutions that last, relationships that grow, and a smarter tomorrow for the next generation.

  • Personal attention

    You deal directly with the people doing the work – no call centres, no runaround.

  • Straight-talking advice

    Clear recommendations in plain language, with honest pricing and no unnecessary extras.

  • Built to last

    Secure, well-documented solutions designed to grow with your business.
